Sponsors / Funding

Support that turns prototypes into launches.

Funding directly enables flight hardware, operations, and student training. We publish transparent mission outcomes, including failures and fixes.

What Funding Enabled

  • RF components: tracker boards, antenna materials, and test equipment.
  • Power + thermal: battery stock, insulation, and environmental testing supplies.
  • Operations: launch consumables, recovery logistics, and safety equipment.
  • Data + software: telemetry workflows and post-flight analysis tooling.

Bill of Materials (Approximate Categories)

  • Payload electronics and wiring
  • Mechanical enclosure and insulation
  • Antenna + RF tuning supplies
  • Flight-day and recovery consumables
