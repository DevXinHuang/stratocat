Project Goal

Build a repeatable student mission architecture for global-scale picoballoon tracking using WSPR telemetry, reliable power, and well-documented launch operations.

Current Mission Status

Mission 2 launched on Sunday, March 22, 2026 and remains in flight. At 8:48 local time (15:48 UTC) on April 4, 2026, the lead Stratocat balloon re-established transmission near 19.48 N, 108.46 W after completing a full global trajectory.

This confirms one full circumnavigation of Earth. The flight is continuing strong, and live tracking remains available as the mission moves beyond its first orbit.
