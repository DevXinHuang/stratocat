Schedule and Milestones

Program timeline from kickoff through the Mission 3 countdown.

Reviews and launches are tracked against fixed milestones to keep mission risk and readiness visible for all teams and sponsors.

September 2025

  • Project kickoff completed.
  • Requirements and success criteria defined.
  • First hardware purchases completed.

October 2025

  • Preliminary Design Review: October 4, 2025.
  • Critical Design Review: October 18, 2025.
  • Flight Readiness Review: October 30, 2025.

November 2025

  • First Launch: November 1, 2025.
  • Post-Flight Review: November 5, 2025.

2026 Operations

  • Launch #2 completed on January 11, 2026.
  • Launch #3 completed on March 22, 2026.
  • First full circumnavigation of Earth confirmed on April 4, 2026.
  • Mission 3 launch window opens April 25, 2026 at 10:00 AM Arizona MST.
